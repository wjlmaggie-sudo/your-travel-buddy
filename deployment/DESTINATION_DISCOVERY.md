# Destination discovery chat

The dedicated **Travel Chatbot** navigation section opens a focused conversation. The planner's **Not sure where? Chat with Travel Buddy** action opens that same section. The chat stays mounted while users switch sections, retaining the current conversation without nested scrolling. Guests can enter rough days, a warmth preference and an activity, answer a follow-up, refine a preference, choose a rough date, or restart. Choosing a destination transfers destination, duration and the closest supported activity category to the existing editable planner. Unspecified group, budget, dates, departure and child details stay intact; explicitly understood details replace the corresponding draft fields after selection. Family discovery also selects the existing Family persona without inventing child counts or ages.

This version is a **free, local guided matcher, not an LLM chatbot**. No API key, provider calls, new dependency or permanent chat history is used. It currently covers 13 destination bases in 12 countries across Europe, Asia, Africa and the Americas, with official visitor source links in src/lib/discovery-destinations.json. Seasonal warmth filters are broad editorial guidance, not live temperature forecasts; dates remain editable and the existing Weather section supplies forecasts when available. Flight length and departure feasibility are not inferred. Unsupported activities get an explicit no-match explanation, not invented attractions, prices or availability. Selection is only a draft change, not a booking or automatic paid search.

The engine and component are separate modules for future collection expansion or an optional server-side model. Existing live flight/hotel searches and their free allowance protections remain unchanged. Regression checks cover multi-turn preference retention, season filtering, unsupported requests, selection into the existing guest hotel flow, family persona, date changes, reset, and preservation of group/budget.

## Structured understanding upgrade — 31 October 2026

Supported messages now extract multiple details in one turn: days/weeks/nights, weather preference, multiple supported activities, excluded activities, departure city, ISO dates or explicit day-month-year dates, adults, children and exact ages, group size, EUR/USD/GBP budgets and per-person budgets. Number words are recognized. Follow-up corrections retain previous details. Nights convert to days plus one. Children with missing ages cause a follow-up; no ages are fabricated. Per-person budgets require a known group before conversion. A **What I understood** card exposes the extracted details, which carry into the existing editable planner only after a destination is selected. Existing unspecified details stay intact. The saved-trip chat starts a new draft without altering the saved trip.

Warmth and activity matching remain curated heuristics over the destination collection, not semantic understanding of every possible request. Budget is carried into the trip form, not treated as a verified destination quote or proof that travel is affordable. Destination cards do not promise live weather, transport availability or prices.

The owner explicitly chose **Keep it strictly free**. No paid AI backend is enabled, no credentials are requested in the frontend, and no provider calls were introduced. The live website and live model inference are different: the website is deployed on GitHub Pages; unrestricted model chat would need a separately hosted model/provider. Optional local open-model inference requires downloads and hardware and would not automatically serve public visitors. OpenAI hosted API usage is billed separately; no such connection has been activated. Documentation: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing .

## Wider input-based suggestions — 31 October 2026

The three-destination limit caused warm hiking/family requests to collapse to Tenerife. The catalog is now independent data with 13 bases: Tenerife, Funchal/Madeira, Lisbon, Chania/Crete, Valletta/Malta, Paphos, Agadir, Dubai, Bali, Cape Town, Tamarindo/Guanacaste and Kyoto, plus Nice. These span 12 countries and four broad world regions. Each entry has supported activity themes, broad editorial warmth months, a suggested minimum stay, caveats and an official visitor source. Seasonal warmth is not a forecast, and family tags never guarantee every trail/activity suits every age.

All requested supported activities, warmth month, explicit region/country and country exclusions are respected together. Examples: “not Spain”, “not in Spain”, “Europe only”, “Asia only”, “prefer Portugal”. “Anywhere” clears a region/country preference but retains explicitly excluded countries. Leading results use different countries where possible; nothing is added solely to make up three results when constraints cannot match. Three cards show initially; **Show more destinations** reveals three more without a new network request. Cards explain activity/season/duration matches and short-trip concerns.

For a known duration and group, cards reuse the existing **broad planner reference allowances**. These are not surveyed destination averages or live quotes. EUR budgets can be compared against the low reference allowance, including the existing generic flight reserve when a departure is supplied. Below-reference budgets get an explicit shortfall; other currencies are not silently converted. No route feasibility or cheap flight is assumed. Destination choices still carry all understood details into the editable planner.

Regression coverage includes multi-country winter suggestions, exact combined constraints, region/country changes, exclusions, low-budget warnings, different-currency handling, progressive disclosure and the existing chat-to-planner flow. No paid model, provider, key or dependency was added.
